Welcome

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at Boston University.

My research interests are in econometrics, with a particular focus on time series econometrics and macroeconometrics.

Before beginning my doctoral studies, I worked as a Junior Economist at the Bank of Korea. I received an M.A. in Economics and a B.S. in Mathematics and a B.A. in Economics from Yonsei University.
